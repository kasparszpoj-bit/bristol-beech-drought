Bristol beech drought project · Phase 1

How Bristol's trees fared in the 2026 drought

Every dot is a large council tree, compared with its own normal summers using Sentinel-2 satellite images, with the grass around it removed. Colour shows the change in red edge, a measure of leaf chlorophyll, in July and August. Zoom in to see each tree's crown, mapped from LIDAR. Click a tree for its history.
